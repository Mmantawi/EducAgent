## Research rules

- Link every important claim to a source page, section, table, or figure.
- Separate demonstrated findings, team interpretation, and proposed implementation decisions.
- Do not compare numerical results across incompatible datasets as though they were directly comparable.
- Document dataset versions, preprocessing, licenses, privacy limits, and intended use.
- Store evaluation procedures and small synthetic fixtures in Git; keep restricted datasets, embeddings, and generated outputs in approved external storage.
- Review copyright and redistribution terms before making source PDFs public or sharing copies outside the project team.
